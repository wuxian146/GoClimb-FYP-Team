How it works

  1. Install GoClimbAR once.
  2. On the website, tap Open in AR camera on any saved wall.
  3. The app opens straight into the camera with that wall's routes loaded, and connects to this website automatically.
  4. Point the phone at the wall. The routes stick to it, even offline once loaded.
